Transparency & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

How CyberSentinel handles audit data, network telemetry, and host privacy.

100% Local Execution

CyberSentinel runs entirely on your local machine or self-hosted container. No audit targets, scan results, or vulnerability telemetry are ever transmitted to third-party cloud servers.

Local SQLite Storage

Historical scans, port listings, and risk scores are persisted locally in a private SQLite database (database.db). You maintain full ownership and control to purge or inspect records at any time.

Data Handling & Security Measures
1. Network Discovery & Target Information

When performing network discovery or port scanning, CyberSentinel only gathers standard TCP/IP response packets, service banners, and OS heuristics from hosts explicitly targeted or located within the user's private subnet.

2. Generated Reports & Exports

Downloadable PDF audit reports generated via ReportLab are compiled locally on the server filesystem. Generated PDF artifacts are stored in the local reports_output/ directory and are not shared or accessible outside your environment.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

CyberSentinel does not use commercial tracking cookies, analytics SDKs, advertising trackers, or fingerprinting scripts. Session state is handled locally without third-party tracking identifiers.

4. External Links

This application contains links to external developer profiles (GitHub, LinkedIn, Instagram, X). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external third-party websites.

Questions Regarding Privacy?

Reach out via GitHub or social channels for inquiries regarding local data handling or methodology.
