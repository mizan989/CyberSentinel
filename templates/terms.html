Terms & Conditions

Rules, authorized scope, and liability boundaries for using CyberSentinel.

Strict Authorization Requirement

CyberSentinel is designed strictly for authorized security research, vulnerability auditing, and educational demonstrations on networks and hosts you own or have explicit written permission to assess.

User Agreement & Operating Principles
1. Non-Intrusive & Read-Only Auditing

CyberSentinel operates exclusively in a read-only capacity. It performs standard TCP SYN/connect scanning, banner discovery, and deterministic CVE signature matching. It does not perform exploit delivery, denial of service (DoS), brute force, or payload injection.

2. Demonstration Allowlist & Sandbox Protection

The platform includes built-in safeguards restricting scans to authorized demonstration hosts (e.g. scanme.nmap.org, localhost) and private RFC-1918 subnets. Attempting to bypass these protections for malicious purposes violates this agreement.
